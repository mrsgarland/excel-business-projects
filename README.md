# CIS 308: Advanced Excel in Business

Welcome! I’m Kristina Garland. My background is in customer service and account resolution, and I enjoy using technology to solve problems and make everyday work easier.

This portfolio documents my learning as I work through Advanced Excel in Business through Arizona State University Universal Learner Courses and apply those skills to original practice projects.

## My Goals

* Organize and analyze business data.
* Use formulas to answer practical questions.
* Create clear charts and reports.
* Connect Excel skills to customer service and account support.

## Skills I Plan to Practice

* Advanced formulas and lookup functions
* Tables and data organization
* Pivot tables
* Charts and data visualization
* What if analysis

## What I Will Share

For each project, I will explain the business question, the Excel tools I used, how I checked my results, and what I learned. I will include screenshots and my own practice workbooks where appropriate.
